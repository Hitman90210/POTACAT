Your QSOs now flow to ARRL Logbook of the World and Club Log, WSPR decoding is back in packaged builds, the Windows installer that could reboot a PC mid-upgrade is neutralized for good, and a stack of rig fixes straight from the week's field reports.

## 🏆 Upload to Logbook of the World

Settings ▸ Logging gains **Upload to LoTW**: POTACAT signs and uploads your whole log through your own TrustedQSL install — pick your TQSL Station Location, add your certificate password only if your cert has one, press Upload. TQSL's duplicate detection means you can press it as often as you like: only QSOs LoTW hasn't seen are sent. Results are reported honestly, including the "nothing new to upload" case.

## 📒 Send QSOs to Club Log

Also in Settings ▸ Logging: enable **Send QSOs to Club Log** and every QSO is pushed to your Club Log account the moment you log it, plus an **Upload entire log now** button for backfill. Authenticate with your Club Log email and an *Application Password* (create one at clublog.org under Settings ▸ Application Passwords — never use your main password; the UI links you straight there).

One honest caveat: Club Log requires a per-application key, and POTACAT's is awaiting approval from the Club Log team. The feature lights up automatically the moment the key is issued — no app update needed. Until then it reports exactly that instead of failing mysteriously.

## 📦 Windows installer: the reboot bug is dead

v1.10.0 shipped an uninstaller with a process-scan bug that could terminate your login session — the "my PC rebooted during the upgrade" reports. The problem is that *upgrades run the old resident uninstaller*, so fixing the new one wasn't enough. This installer now detects the bad uninstaller version and neutralizes it before it can run, on every install path it may live in. Upgrading from any version, including 1.10.0 itself, is now safe.

## 📡 WSPR decoding restored

Packaged builds were shipping without the `wsprd` decoder entirely — WSPR listened and heard nothing. The WSJT-X 2.7.0 decoder source (including the Fortran deep-search OSD stage) is now vendored and compiled for every platform in CI, so packaged WSPR decodes like the real thing again.

## 🔧 Rig fixes from the field

- **Icom IC-7100** — the preamp is a two-stage ladder now: Off → P.AMP1 → P.AMP2. Pre 2 was unreachable.
- **Icom meters on voice** — S/SWR/ALC tracking follows microphone keying, so the meters live during SSB TX, not just app-initiated transmissions. The Settings sidebar also scrolls on short windows.
- **CW via DTR key port** — a configured serial key line now wins over CAT-side CW text on rigs that support both (an IC-756 Pro III NAKed the CAT route).
- **Flex + AetherSDR** — the TX-slice flag is asserted before DAX TX, fixing "transmitter keys but no RF" when another client had moved it.
- **Kenwood split (TS-480 and friends)** — with SPLIT on, the VFO window and mobile devices show *both* frequencies, RX over TX.
- **rigctld** — decimal frequency and S-meter replies (wfview) are parsed instead of rejected.
- **CAT link health** — a link whose every poll is rejected now backs off and names the likely cause once, instead of flooding the log with `?;` errors forever.
- **SWR guard** — tripping the guard with auto-tune enabled no longer crashes the app.
- **Linux audio** — raw ALSA devices are capture-only and no longer offered as *output* devices (nothing could play to them); a previously-saved ALSA output now gets an accurate explanation instead of a false "radio will NOT transmit" warning.
- **Remote Launcher** — Stop terminates the whole process tree, and a rapid refresh no longer briefly renders a running station as STOPPED.
- **VFO pop-out** — a blocked tune now says *why* it's blocked, and the lock state is correct from the moment the window opens.
- **CW macros** — "Remove Last" is always available in the macro editor.

## 🧭 Menu rework: modes up front

The More menu now says what it means: **FT8 / FT4 / FT2**, **WSPR**, **PSK31**, and **JS8** are each their own entry — the "JTCAT" label is gone from the menu. The idle-station section in Settings is now called **Idle Mode** and includes JS8 alongside WSPR, PSK31, and SSTV.
