**Headline:** POTACAT's FT8 decoder now finds 97% of what WSJT-X finds on the same recordings, and its SNR reports are measured the way WSJT-X measures them. Also: a Contests page that stays up to date by itself, an Activation window with a shareable image, and a round of SSTV, CW and ECHOCAT fixes.

## FT8 decodes more, and reports honest SNR

Thanks to Ray for the side-by-side comparison with WSJT-X (#87).

- **More decodes:** POTACAT now makes up to three passes over each FT8 period. After each pass it subtracts the stations it has decoded, so stations underneath or beside a strong signal can be decoded too. It also uses WSJT-X's finer demodulation on signals the first look can't crack. On the ft8_lib test recordings, which come with WSJT-X's own decodes, POTACAT went from 933 to **1251 of 1289**, including 112 of the 124 at -20 dB or below. It made no false decodes on 100 periods of pure noise.
- **SNR is now measured correctly:** SNR is now taken from the decoded signal itself, as signal power over the noise in 2500 Hz, which is WSJT-X's definition. The old estimate missed a station that was even half a hertz off, so it often read several dB low. That number is also **the signal report JTCAT sends**.
- **No more silent stops:** on a busy band, after about 15 minutes of new callsigns, the decoder's callsign table could fill up and decoding would stop without any message. The table now makes room as it goes.
- **Hunt no longer skips new contacts** (N2FSM): a station you'd worked earlier in the session on one band or mode was skipped on every other band and mode until POTACAT restarted. It is now skipped only on the band and mode you actually worked it on.

## Contests: redone, and kept current from our server

- **The page:** titled "Contests", with one line per contest in columns: status, contest and sponsor, start in UTC, length, modes, bands, notes and a Rules link.
- **Grouping:** "Later" is split by month, so annual events like Route 66 are easy to find.
- **Modes filter:** a new filter for CW, Phone and Digital. A contest shows if any of its modes is ticked, so a CW + SSB QSO party still shows for phone operators.
- **180 contests and events**, each date checked against its sponsor's own rules:
  - 98 added, including Lewis & Clark Trail On The Air, the WWFF August anniversary, SKCC Straight Key Month, ARRL Kids Day and School Club Roundup, 27 more state and province QSO parties, and the big FT8/FT4 contests.
  - Ten wrong dates fixed. The Texas QSO Party is one of them: it showed as live a week late.
  - Two events that no longer exist removed.
- **Kept current:** our server now updates the contest dates every day and checks every sponsor link weekly. POTACAT picks up the update within six hours, so corrections reach you without a new release.

## Activation window, and an image to share

Asked for by Ken NO4D.

- **The Activation window:** the activation map is now an Activation window, with your log beside the map. Switch between Map, Log or both. It counts your contacts toward the 10 a POTA activation needs, and it follows your edits and deletes.
- **From the FT8 window:** it shows an **Activation** button while you're activating, so you can watch your log without leaving FT8.
- **Save image / Copy:** makes a picture of the activation for Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and similar. It shows your callsign, the park and where it is, the date, QSOs, park-to-parks and states, bands and modes.
- **Two sizes:** **Post** (4:5) and **Story** (9:16). The text is kept inside each platform's safe zone.

## SSTV

Thanks to WB8IMY.

- **Wrong-mode locks:** picture content could be mistaken for a mode header (for example PD-160 or Robot 24), and the wrong lock was held for the whole length of that mode. The next real transmission's header was ignored too. A wrong lock is now dropped within a few seconds, and a new header in the middle of a picture restarts on the right mode. Image quality on correct decodes is unchanged.
- **Icom transmit:** SSTV on an Icom now transmits in USB-D. In plain USB, the IC-7300's factory settings take transmit audio from the mic, so the radio keyed with no audio.

## CW

Thanks to LZ3AW.

- **ECHOCAT Web sidetone:** the local sidetone for the paddle and for key-as-you-type now keeps exact dot, dash and space timing, the same way macros always did. It used to sound choppy.
- **TS-480 filter:**
  - Clicking from one CW spot to another no longer puts the radio on an SSB filter width.
  - The VFO window now shows the real CW filter width.
- **TS-480, TS-590, TS-890 and TS-990 paddles:** paddle elements no longer switch the radio into transmit over CAT on top of the real keying. That push-to-talk could cut an over a few seconds in.

## ECHOCAT

- **Pairing:** if ECHOCAT was turned on after POTACAT started, a pairing request waited for an approval window that never appeared until POTACAT restarted. Fixed.
- **After the ECHOCAT server restarts:** Guest Pass sign-ins no longer fail, and the VFO lock no longer shows as unlocked.
- **New installs:** ECHOCAT Web doesn't ask for a token. When a token is used, it has no look-alike characters such as 5 and S.
- **Mic gain on ECHOCAT Web** (K6RBJ): a Mic Gain slider in Settings and a MIC row in the full VFO pane set the radio's own mic gain, for radios that report it. The ECHOCAT app already had it.
- **Rate ECHOCAT:** a one-time card asks mobile users for a store review, at a calm moment and never while you're operating.

## Also fixed

- **Spot list** (N0KAH): a spot at a different park on your frequency is no longer drawn as if it were part of the same activation. It gets its own dashed amber edge.
- **Spot clicks and filter width** (#85): a click in the same mode family leaves your filter width alone. The FTDX-1200 went to 2500 on every click.
- **Hamlib rigctld through wfview** (#82): preamp and attenuator commands now find a value the radio accepts.
- **WebSDR CW** (#93, DF1VB): the WebSDR now shows the station on its own frequency, not 600 Hz low.
- **LoTW uploads** (#92): a skipped QSO now shows which one it was and TQSL's reason, instead of "see log".
- **Logbook** (#94): the delete confirmation is readable and always in view.
- **External ATU** (#90): the tune carrier can be set as low as 1 W.
- **New installs:** light mode by default, charcoal as the default dark theme, and WSPR as the idle mode.
- **What's New:** formatting in release notes now displays properly instead of showing raw characters.
