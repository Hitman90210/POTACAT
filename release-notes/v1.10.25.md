**Headline:** POTA spots now match pota.app, the ECHOCAT paddle sends what you key, and an IC-7610 in Standby wakes up from POTACAT. Plus fixes for KiwiSDR CW tuning, the Remote Launcher, watchlist colours and Auto-RX.

## POTA spots match pota.app

Thanks to Mike N4FFF.

- **Every active activator:** POTACAT only showed POTA spots from the last 5 minutes. pota.app lists an activator until their spot expires, which can be half an hour, so on a quiet morning POTACAT showed about half of them. POTA spots now stay until pota.app drops them, on the desktop and in ECHOCAT.
- **Your choice:** Settings > Spot Filters > "Apply the max spot age to POTA too" brings back the old cut. WWFF, LLOTA and WWBOTA keep the max spot age, because their feeds keep spots for hours.
- **One setting:** a max spot age changed from ECHOCAT now reaches the desktop spot list too.

## CW

Thanks to LZ3AW.

- **ECHOCAT paddle:** while you hold a paddle, ECHOCAT sends a "still held" message so POTACAT knows the contact is down. POTACAT treated each one as a new press, so the radio could send an extra dit or dah. The sidetone never heard those messages, so it sounded right while the radio sent something else. Fixed.
- **No double sidetone on ECHOCAT Web:** POTACAT mixed its own copy of your CW text into the receive audio, on top of the one the web page plays, so you heard it twice. It no longer does that for text you send from the web. The ECHOCAT app still gets it.
- **Wattmeter:** the power meter is now read every second while you transmit, and it holds the peak like the radio's own bar. It used to catch CW between elements and read low.

## Radios and receivers

- **IC-7610 power on** (and other Icoms with a Standby power-off setting): Rig On from POTACAT or ECHOCAT now wakes the radio. A sleeping Icom needs a burst of wake-up bytes before the power-on command, and POTACAT didn't send them.
- **KiwiSDR CW** (#93, DF1VB): in CW, a KiwiSDR tuned exactly onto the spot, which put the station outside the listening window. You only heard yourself 600 Hz higher. It now tunes 600 Hz below the spot, so the station plays as a 600 Hz tone. WebSDR.org sites now also get the mode the way their own page sends it, so AM and FM no longer tune as SSB.

## FT8

- **Two QSOs in one message:** some operators answer two stations in one transmission, like `K3SBP RR73; AI5MM <AF0E> -14`. An RR73 to you in the first half now completes your QSO. Before, POTACAT kept sending its report.

## ECHOCAT

- **Auto-RX shows the real mode:** when the desktop was idle-listening on WSPR, PSK31 or JS8, ECHOCAT showed "SSTV armed" and an AUTO-SSTV banner. It now names the mode that's running.
- **Auto-RX stops when you take over:** tuning, starting FT8 or changing mode from ECHOCAT, or closing the window Auto-RX opened, now ends the idle session. It used to linger for hours.

## Also fixed

- **Remote Launcher Stop** (#84, officiallor): Stop now shuts POTACAT down instead of only closing its window. It closes POTACAT normally first, so settings are saved and the radio is released.
- **Watchlist colours** (#89, khoogheem): watchlist groups now highlight spots from the moment POTACAT starts. Before, they only appeared after you opened Settings.
- **Mac:** the X on a contest's info panel closes it again.
