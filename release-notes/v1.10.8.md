The LoTW integration goes full circle — uploaded marks, paper QSLs, and confirmation downloads feeding the DXCC tracker. The WSPR beacon stops being a black box. And an eagle-eyed user's log spelunking got the America 250 WAS tracker set right for everyone.

## 🏆 LoTW comes full circle

- **Every uploaded QSO is marked.** After each LoTW upload, QSOs get `LOTW_QSL_SENT` stamped into the log — except the ones TQSL skipped, which stay unmarked so you know what needs fixing. Already-uploaded duplicates are marked too, so your first upload on 1.10.8 backfills your whole history.
- **Download your confirmations.** Enter your LoTW website login (not the certificate password — the UI keeps them straight) and press Download confirmations: every QSL LoTW has issued gets matched to your log. First run pulls full history; later runs fetch only what's new.
- **The QSO log window gains a QSL column**: `L` = uploaded, `L✓` = LoTW confirmed, `P` = paper card received — double-click to toggle the paper mark. Confirmed QSOs count as confirmed in the DXCC Tracker automatically.
- Two polish fixes from the first field reports: a huge log with many skipped QSOs no longer kills TQSL mid-run, and failures name their real cause instead of "exit -1".

## 📡 WSPR beacon: no more black box

The beacon's random TX percentage meant it could legitimately sit silent for its first ten minutes after you armed it — indistinguishable from broken, and reported as such. Three changes:
- **The first slot after arming always transmits.** Check Beacon TX and you get RF within two minutes, confirming the whole chain; the randomized collision-avoidance draw takes over from there.
- The WSPR bar shows each slot's decision live: **TX this slot** or **listening this slot**.
- The arm message states the expected cadence, and the WSPR distance column is readable in light mode.

## 🗽 America 250 WAS: states set right

A user's careful log spelunking caught two real bugs: the banner's call-district table had districts 8 and 0 transposed ("Ohio (W1AW/0)"), and the log stamper credited every W1AW suffix to the first state listed each week — so multi-state weeks mis-stamped for everyone (a /7 Oregon contact tagged as New Jersey). Both fixed — and the schedule feed was corrected server-side, so **stamping already self-healed on every version** within hours of the report. What 1.10.8 adds: the corrected banner, refuse-to-guess matching, and a repair pass — press **"Stamp past QSOs"** once and every mis-stamped QSO in your log and WAS board gets recomputed and fixed.

## 🚀 Remote Launcher: boring at last

- The status board gets its own rate-limit budget, so clicking buttons can never starve the display into showing stale state (#70).
- Stop no longer reports a failure for a stop that worked — the launcher now waits for the processes to actually exit before judging (#80).
- Restart the launcher itself once after updating to pick up these fixes.
